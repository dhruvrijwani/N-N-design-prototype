# Print stories (final copy from the client, 16 November 2026)

Context: each box contains a spin-wheel game with Lumi the elephant at the centre; the wheel shows situations, and parents can make up their own. A QR code on the packaging / thank-you card lands on the Prints page, which tells these stories. The three stories run in sequence: Under One Moon → Dream Atlas → Hush Hush.

## UNDER ONE MOON
Every night, a little elephant gazed at the sky.
"Mama, why is the sky so high?"
"I want to play with the stars!"
He stretched his trunk.
Up, up, UP!
"Papa, lift me! Higher! HIGHER!"
Papa gave a little wobble.
"Any higher and I'll tickle the moon!"
But the stars were still too far away.
"Will they always be so far?" sighed the little elephant.
Mama kissed his head.
"Oh, little one. The sky is full of surprises."
The elephant looked up again.
"Well, if I can't climb up…"
He lifted his trunk and called,
"HELLO, STARS! COME AND PLAY!"
Night after night, he called.
He waved. He jumped. He even stood on one leg!
But the stars stayed high.
Or so he thought…
For one little star had begun to twinkle back.
First, a tiny twinkle.
Then a brighter blink.
Until one night, it wiggled.
It wobbled.
And gave a great big…
WHEEEEE!
Down it floated!
Plink! Right onto his trunk!
"MAMA! PAPA! LOOK!"
The moon leaned closer.
Another star followed. Then another!
Soon, the sky was dancing all around him.
The little elephant laughed and twirled beneath the moon.
He had wanted to play with the stars.
And all along, one little star had wanted to play with him, too.
That night, tucked between Mama and Papa, he whispered,
"Tomorrow, I'm telling all my friends!"

## DREAM ATLAS
The little elephant had a wonderful secret.
"A star came down to play with me!"
"Really?" gasped Bunny.
"Can we meet one too?"
They waved and called.
But the stars stayed high.
"Then let's go UP!" declared Teddy.
"Climb aboard!"
WHOOOOSH!
His little aeroplane soared into the sky.
Past twinkling stars and fluffy clouds.
All the way to the moon!
Bunny hopped onto its silver crescent.
"Look! I'm fishing for stars!"
Plink! Plink! went his little fishing line.
Nearby, Bird sang a happy tune.
But Teddy had flown into a cloud.
POOF!
Out he came with a fluffy white moustache!
Everyone giggled.
Then another cloud drifted over.
POOF!
Now his aeroplane looked like a giant marshmallow!
"Teddy!" laughed Bunny. "You look delicious!"
But the clouds kept drifting and drifting.
Soon, Teddy couldn't find the moon.
"Bunny? Which way is back?"
Bunny looked around.
Then he spotted something.
A tiny twinkle peeking through the clouds.
Blink. Blink. BLINK!
Bunny remembered Elephant's story.
"Perhaps that little star can help!"
He cast his fishing line towards the twinkle.
SWISH!
The little star caught the line and gave a playful tug.
"This way!" called Bunny.
Teddy followed the glowing line.
Bird sang from the moon to guide them.
And out from the clouds came…
TEDDY!
"HOORAY!"
Teddy hugged Bunny.
"You found my way back!"
Bunny grinned.
"And you helped me reach the moon!"
Far below, Elephant waved his trunk.
He knew that happy little twinkle.
It was his starry friend!
And beneath the smiling moon, the friends headed home, with a sky full of stories to tell.

## HUSH HUSH
Teddy had been on the grandest adventure!
He had flown past the moon, danced through the clouds and waved to the twinkling stars.
But now it was bedtime.
And Teddy was wide awake!
He closed his eyes.
But his paws were still flying.
WHOOSH! went one paw.
WHOOSH! went the other.
"Oh dear!" he sighed. "My adventures haven't finished yet!"
A fluffy cloud floating nearby heard him.
"Perhaps I can help!" it whispered.
The cloud puffed itself up.
Puff, puff, PUFF!
Until it became the softest, fluffiest bed in the sky.
Teddy snuggled in.
"Ahhh! Just right!"
But his little eyes were still wide open.
Two tiny stars peeked down.
"Shall we sing?" whispered one.
And together, they began…
Hush, hush, little bear,
Dreams are floating everywhere.
Rest your paws and close your eyes,
We'll watch over sleepy skies.
The cloud swayed gently.
To and fro.
Slowly, softly.
Teddy's paws grew still.
His eyes grew heavy.
"Will you still be here tomorrow?" he whispered.
"Every night," murmured the cloud.
One little star gave a final twinkle.
The other was already fast asleep.
Teddy smiled, closed his eyes…
And dreamed of his next big adventure.
Hush, hush, little dreamer. The sky is yours tonight.
