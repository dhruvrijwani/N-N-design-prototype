Source: client email, forwarded by Dhruv, plus Dhruv's notes.

## Dhruv's notes
- Catalogue: 4 products. AeroCool Sleepsuit (3 prints), AeroCool PJ Set (3 prints), and two sock products: **Cloud Hopper** and **Little Wild Trio**. List each print as its own product, connected by sibling metafields. No dummy products.
- Our story and Our approach: complete revamp. Client finds them "AI-sloppy", boxy, ecommerce sections with plain content added.
- New Bundles page (configurable). Collection page revamp.
- Same colours; keep 2 fonts; font sizing matters a lot; far less italic.
- Redesign product cards, sitemap and navigation.
- Section count is the client's call, not ours.
- Client disliked how "the result" was highlighted in the fabric section (taupe rounded pill).
- Always check alignment.
- Stiletto presets are classy; current palette feels dull because of how it is used.
- New: Prints page (QR landing from the spin-wheel game in the box). See 02-print-stories.md.

## Client email (verbatim)

Shopping sections: More product-led, visually prominent and easier to browse, with products, prices and CTAs clearly standing out.
AeroCool technology: More visual and interactive, using diagrams or illustrations/ videos rather than text-heavy explanations or PPT-style layouts.
Product benefits: Use visual feature highlights, icons or garment-detail close-ups where appropriate, rather than repeating editorial text blocks.
Brand/founder storytelling: This is where the editorial style works well and can remain.
Bundles: Should feel like an interactive shopping experience, not another editorial section.

The idea is not to redesign the website or add unnecessary visual elements, but to establish a better hierarchy and rhythm so that the different sections feel distinct and purposeful.

The primary objective remains sales and conversion, supported by a brand experience that makes parents feel understood and supported—without excessive storytelling or content.

### Homepage
- How AeroCool Works: Simplify to two visual states—when temperatures rise and when they fall—with short explanations. The current presentation feels like PPT bullet points. Please explore a more engaging visual treatment that makes the technology easy to understand at a glance. Some statements may change later based on our testing results.
- Shop Their Bedtime: As discussed, please highlight each of our three prints as an individual shoppable product presentation under the relevant garment categories. With a limited initial product range, we want the illustrations and prints to stand out, rather than showing only one generic Sleepsuit and PJ Set.
- Bundles: Replace the current three bundle concepts with:
  - Bedtime Rotation: Any two sleepwear garments.
  - Bedtime & Beyond: Two sleepwear garments + choice of Little Wild Trio or Cloud Hopper grip socks.
  - Both offer 7% savings (subject to final unit economics).
  - Homepage cards should link to the dedicated configurable bundle page.
- Journal: Reduce the four article previews to two.
- Page length: Remove repeated explanations of temperature regulation and product benefits. Avoid introducing additional sections that communicate the same message.

### AeroCool Product / Shopping Pages
- Product presentation: We want each of the three prints—Under One Moon, Dream Atlas and Hush Hush—to be individually highlighted and shoppable within the Sleepsuit and PJ Set collections. This gives us six distinct product presentations while retaining the two garment categories. Please ensure customers can easily browse by print as well as garment type.
- Size selection: Show a clear selected state, unavailable sizes and an accessible size guide with actual garment measurements (to be populated during development).
- Purchase section: Add a contextual CTA below Add to Bag: "Build your bundle & save 7%."
- Product description: Remove repetition between the introductory description, fabric explanation and seven construction features. Each benefit should be explained clearly in one place.
- Print stories: Display the story corresponding to the selected print first, with a CTA to read it. Other print stories can remain secondary (QR will be on the thank you card).

### Our Approach
- Shorten the opening problem and inspiration sections, as they currently repeat the same temperature-change explanation.
- Make the four-stage PCM explanation more visual and easier to follow, with one concise explanation per stage.
- Add a Shop AeroCool CTA immediately after the PCM explanation.
- Avoid repeating all seven garment features already presented on the product page.
- Differentiate the visual treatment of the science/technology sections from the editorial storytelling sections. This page should feel more like a simple explanation of our product innovation.

### Our Story
- Reduce the overall copy by approximately 35–40%, particularly the repeated sections about parenting questions and guesswork.
- Condense the founder narrative into a clear progression: personal experience → recurring parenting problems → why Nurture & Next was created.
- Remove the three Purpose / Belief / Positioning cards. These feel more like internal brand statements than customer-facing content.
- Reduce repeated oversized statement sections and make the founder story more conversational.
- End with one clear Shop CTA.

### Library
- Replace Collection A and Collection B with Lumi's Wonder Trails and Little Lessons for Growing Minds.
- Reduce each book description to one short, engaging sentence.
- For available books, use Buy on Amazon, linking directly to the respective book.

### Contact Us / Help Centre
- Shorten the Contact page by keeping the contact form and a short FAQ section. Move detailed Shipping & Returns information to their respective pages.
- Display clickable customer-support email and WhatsApp details, with confirmed support hours.
- Prioritise FAQs on sizing, delivery, exchanges and order tracking.
- Ensure the contact form has clear submission confirmation and error messages.

### Site-wide
- Set UAE/AED as the default storefront. Remove USD and United States placeholders.
- Remove Bedtime Bags for now.
- Please ensure the same editorial layout isn't repeated throughout the website. We want a better balance of visual storytelling, product merchandising, informative content and interactive shopping sections.
