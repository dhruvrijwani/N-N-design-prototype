Custom code.

Print colours
A small CSS snippet that reads the print metafield and colours print cards, swatches and banners.
AeroCool diagram
A section with the two-state SVG and toggle. Copy editable in the theme editor.
AeroCool stepper
A section with four blocks (drawing + line). Falls back to a native multi-column if needed.
Spin wheel and story reader
Two sections; wheel situations and story pages edited as metaobjects.
Bundle cards, delivery table
Already prepared in the theme folder.
Garment switch
A snippet in the product info that links to the twin product.

Apps.

Bundles
A mix-and-match app (e.g. Fast Bundle, Kaching Bundles, Simple Bundles) for Bedtime Rotation and Bedtime & Beyond at 7%.
Search & Discovery
Free, by Shopify: filters.
Reviews
Judge.me or similar, turned on once there are real reviews.
Email
Shopify Email or Klaviyo for the newsletter.

Content to prepare.

  1. Print artwork files (vector) for Under One Moon, Dream Atlas and Hush Hush, and a Lumi illustration.
  2. Product photography per product and print, clean, at least four each.
  3. Garment measurements for every size.
  4. The wheel’s situations, exactly as printed.
  5. Prices, bundle discount, contact details, support hours.

Sequence.

  1. Client review of prototype v2, feedback round.
  2. Store setup: AED market, shipping, policies, apps, metafields and collections.
  3. Theme: settings, then the shopping path (home, shop, product, bundles, bag), then stories, AeroCool, our story, help.
  4. Products and content, then testing on real phones and a test order.
