Design review

The content is strong. The design was unfinished, and it showed.

The previous Figma file has good copy, a clear product story and a full set of brand photographs. It stopped before the work that makes a store launchable: phone layouts, a collection page, a bag, one consistent price list, and finished facts. This prototype keeps the content and layout, fixes what was broken, and builds every page in Stiletto’s own sections.

The ten that matter most.

  1. No phone design. All 30 frames are 1440px desktop. Most visitors will arrive on a phone, from Instagram. Every page in the prototype works at 390px.
  2. Two currencies and two sets of names. The home page sells the sleepsuit for $54 in “Moonlit Meadow” and “Starlit Sky”; the product page sells it for AED 349 in “Under One Moon”, “Dream Atlas” and “Hush Hush”. The prototype uses AED and the product page’s names everywhere.
  3. Pages that buttons lead to were never designed. Collection, bag and journal. All three are built here from Stiletto templates.
  4. Reviews and videos for a store with no customers. A quote marked “01 / 05” and five “customer video” tiles, before launch. Both are kept in layout but flagged until real ones exist.
  5. Unfinished facts shown to customers. “[To be added]”, “Approved measurements coming soon” (four times), “0.5 TOG — testing to be verified”, “Proposed launch policy”, and a testing line on the home page.
  6. Photos with words and logos inside them. Many images are social posts with captions baked in, which look like errors on a web page. All were cropped clean for the prototype; clean originals are needed.
  7. A photo that contradicts the story. “A little boy. A big life change.” sits beside a photo of a girl.
  8. An empty image placeholder on the story page, and the same hero photo on two pages.
  9. Bundle maths that does not add up. “Any three pieces from $75” is cheaper than two sleepsuits at $54. Bundle prices need setting in AED.
  10. Amazon first. The library section’s main button sends visitors to Amazon. The prototype swaps it to the brand’s own library.

Page by page.

Home
Five versions in the file; the latest (12 sections) was used. Kept in order. Fixed: currency, names, age labels, testing line, Amazon priority, footer country. Flagged: videos, review, bundle prices.
Product
Four versions; the longest (7,499px) was used. Strong content. Fixed: product name, print selection, all seven details laid out, internal policy labels. Flagged: TOG, size chart, testing.
Our story
Founder’s story, kept word for word. Fixed: empty placeholder, girl photo beside “a little boy”, repeated hero photo. Flagged: founder name and photo.
The fabric
Five chapters, kept. Fixed: page name, the four “how it works” icons. Flagged: testing chapter.
Library
Two versions; the series names and facts from the earlier one were restored. Book covers taken from the file at full sharpness. Flagged: home tiles name different books.
Help & contact
Kept. Fixed: eleven FAQ answers written from the brand’s own policy text. Flagged: contact details, payment methods, first-order discount.
Shop, Bag, Journal
Not designed; built from Stiletto templates in the same style.
