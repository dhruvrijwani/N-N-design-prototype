The same seven colours and two typefaces, used with more contrast and stricter rules. Shopping sections are product-first, the science is visual, and the editorial voice is kept for the founder and the stories.

Same palette, sharper roles.

The site felt dull because mid-tones sat on mid-tones: olive headings and taupe text on cream. Now text is charcoal, products sit on white, and colour arrives through the prints.

Primary buttons are now charcoal. The brand’s taupe button stays for softer actions; white text on taupe is 2.6:1, too low for the button that takes payment.

Each print owns a colour.

The prints are the product, so each gets a field that follows it everywhere: print cards, swatches, collection banners, story pages. The drawings are placeholders until the print artwork files arrive.

Nine styles, nothing else.

Newsreader for headings, product names and quotes. DM Sans for everything else. Sentence case everywhere; capitals only for small labels and buttons. Italic only in the print stories and in quotes.

Four kinds of section, each looking different.

So the site has rhythm instead of the same two-column block on every section.

Shop

Product-first. White tiles, print colours, price and buttons always visible. Home tabs, collection, product, bundles.

Science

A diagram in a tinted field, one line per state or stage, interactive. AeroCool on the home page and its own page.

Story

Editorial: large photo or illustration, conversational copy, the serif. Founder story, print stories, the wheel.

Service

Compact and scannable. Help, shipping, exchanges, size guide.

Buttons

Product card
4:5 photo on white; second photo fades in on hover; at most one badge; “+” opens a size picker inside the card; name and price on one line; print dot and swatches below.
Print card

Size chips
White; selected is charcoal; sold out is crossed through and cannot be chosen.
Motion
Stiletto’s own: one easing curve, 300ms for hovers, 500ms for reveals. Sections fade up once as they enter. No bounce, no zoom-in on load.
Grid
1360px maximum width, 20 / 32 / 48px side margins, 16–24px gaps. Text, images and buttons share the same left edge.
