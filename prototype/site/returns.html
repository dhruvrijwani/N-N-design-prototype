Help / Exchanges & returns

Need a different size?

Little ones grow quickly. AeroCool sleepwear can be exchanged for another size within 7 days of delivery in the UAE.

  1. Message us

    Within 7 days of delivery, with your order number and the size you need.

  2. Send it back

    Unworn, unwashed, tags on. You cover the return; no need to send the gift box or Lumi’s wheel.

  3. We send the new size

    We cover delivery of the replacement, for one exchange per order.

What can be exchanged?
AeroCool sleepsuits and PJ sets, for a different size, subject to availability.
What can’t be exchanged?
Grip socks, for hygiene reasons, except where the law requires otherwise.
Can I return for a refund?
We don’t offer refunds for change of mind. This never limits your rights if an item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered.
International orders
Size exchanges and returns aren’t available outside the UAE at launch. Your statutory rights are not affected.
