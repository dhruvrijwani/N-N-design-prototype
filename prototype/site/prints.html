How to play

A bedtime ritual in three steps

  1. Step oneSpin

    Let them spin Lumi’s wheel from the box, or the one on this page.

  2. Step twoTell

    Say where Lumi is going tonight. They decide what happens next.

  3. Step threeTuck in

    Finish with Lumi’s book for that adventure, read slowly, lights low.
