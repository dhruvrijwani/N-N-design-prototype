How AeroCool works

Fabric that adjusts while they sleep

It absorbs extra warmth

Tiny capsules in the yarn soak up excess heat and store it, drawing it away from their skin.

It gives that warmth back

As the room cools, the stored heat is released, so there’s no sudden chill.
