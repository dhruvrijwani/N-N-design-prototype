Which size should I choose?

Go by height and weight rather than age. Each product page has a size guide with the garment measurements. Between sizes? WhatsApp us their height and weight and we’ll suggest one.

How long does delivery take?
Dubai: next business day for orders placed by 5 PM. Other emirates: 2–4 business days. GCC: about 3–7 business days.
How much is delivery?
AED 30 in the UAE, free on orders over AED 500. International rates are shown at checkout.
Can I exchange for a different size?
Yes, for AeroCool sleepwear in the UAE, within 7 days of delivery, unworn and with tags. Grip socks can’t be exchanged, for hygiene reasons. How exchanges work
How do I track my order?
We email a tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also find it under your order in your account.
How do I wash AeroCool sleepwear?
Mild machine wash at 30°C, inside out. Line dry, or tumble dry up to 60°C.
